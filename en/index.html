OUR POSITION

What young people need to learn in the age of AI.

Generative AI has made knowledge, content and answers easier to reach than ever. When anyone can produce an essay, a report, an image or code in seconds, young people need these abilities all the more:

  • Ask good questions and go looking for answers
  • Judge how reliable information, evidence and AI output are
  • Turn abstract knowledge into real action
  • Make responsible choices under uncertainty
  • Work, communicate and create with people from different backgrounds
  • Correct course after mistakes, feedback and setbacks
  • See the value they can create for their family, community, companies, country and the world

Starting today, young people can use real action to build the capability, work, relationships and direction worth carrying forward.
