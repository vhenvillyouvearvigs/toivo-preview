OUR POSITION

What young people need to learn in the age of AI.

Generative AI has made knowledge, content and answers easier to reach than ever. When anyone can produce an essay, a report, an image or code in seconds, young people need these abilities all the more:

  1. 01 Ask good questions and go looking for answers
  2. 02 Judge how reliable information, evidence and AI output are
  3. 03 Turn abstract knowledge into real action
  4. 04 Make responsible choices under uncertainty
  5. 05 Work, communicate and create with people from different backgrounds
  6. 06 Correct course after mistakes, feedback and setbacks
  7. 07 See the value they can create for their family, community, companies, country and the world

Starting today, young people can use real action to build the capability, work, relationships and direction worth carrying forward.

TOIVO Pathways

The five-step loop

Brief, Build, Feedback, Refine, Verify. Every step leaves one artifact, and the artifacts accumulate into an Evidence Pack. After the fifth step comes the next opportunity.

IN DEVELOPMENT · 2026-08

  1. Brief

    Define the problem, audience, limits and assessment criteria together with the partner.

    ARTIFACT 01 / 05

    Task Brief

  2. Build

    Research, interviews, bilingual comms, AI collaboration and a first prototype.

    ARTIFACT 02 / 05

    Problem Map

  3. Feedback

    The partner and beneficiaries respond at midpoint and again at final review.

    ARTIFACT 03 / 05

    Partner Feedback

  4. Refine

    Compare the feedback, revise, and explain every trade-off and judgement call.

    ARTIFACT 04 / 05

    Revision & Decision Log

  5. Verify

    Mentor and partner verify the work against the rubric. The pack is sealed and a digital credential is issued.

    ARTIFACT 05 / 05

    Evidence Pack
