PRINCIPLES AND COMMITMENTS

Responsible AI

The stronger the technology, the more human agency matters.

This page states the beliefs, design commitments and rules TOIVO has set for its product.

Core beliefs Seven commitments Technical principles Data and access

Core beliefs

AI should extend people’s capacity to think, learn, connect and choose. Judgement, relationships and growth remain in human hands.

We design AI as learning scaffolding with clear limits:

  • Helping students clarify the question; the answer is theirs to find
  • Helping students compare information; the conclusion is theirs to judge
  • Helping students take the first step; the task is theirs to complete
  • Helping teachers see where students are stuck; the judgement stays with the teacher
  • Helping parents see their child’s growth while respecting the child’s private space
  • Helping principals drive school-based innovation, with every analysis explainable and open to checking

Seven commitments

These are the design commitments we have set for the product, which is still in development.

  1. Students think first, then AI supports

    Students should first form their own question, hypothesis, draft or idea. Only then does AI offer hints, examples, structure, fact-checking and feedback.

  2. Teachers keep professional authority

    AI can draft, organise, prompt and assist. Teaching, assessment, guidance, discipline and high-impact decisions always remain the responsibility of teachers and qualified professionals.

  3. No labelling of students

    TOIVO does not generate fixed labels such as “low-motivation student”, “problem student”, “not suited to this subject” or “high-risk personality”.

    The system may only show learning patterns that can be explained and corrected, for example:

    “In recent tasks the student has often stopped at the first step, and may need clearer task steps or success criteria.”

  4. No mental health diagnosis or life prediction

    TOIVO will not use a student’s conversations, assignments, clicks, login times, voice, images or emotional writing to diagnose anxiety, depression, ADHD, addiction, personality or family problems.

    Design requirement: when a student says they are struggling, the platform offers ways to get help and refers them to a real person. It does not replace counselling, medical or clinical services.

  5. Students own their work and what they share

    Step by step, students can understand, manage, add to, correct and choose to share their own work, digital credentials, Evidence Passport and growth records.

  6. People review high-impact decisions

    High-impact decisions on admissions, discipline, special educational needs, psychological support, financial aid, recruitment, allocation of opportunities or data sharing must have clearly defined permissions, human review and an appeal process.

  7. No addictive design to drive usage

    TOIVO does not compete for students’ attention with infinite scroll, late-night push notifications, login streaks, loot boxes or public rankings.

    What we value:

    • Completing meaningful tasks
    • Knowing when to stop
    • Returning to real relationships
    • Offline observation, interviews, experiments, sport and service
    • Sleep, recovery and a sustainable pace of learning

DESIGN PRINCIPLES · 2026-09

TOIVO’s technology platform is designed on the four principles below. Controlled model routing is already in use; the rest of the platform is not built yet.

PrincipleDesign requirement

Replaceable models

Design requirement

Controlled model routing chooses established model services task by task, balancing quality, cost and safety. The design allows providers to be changed to meet performance, data classification and regional requirements.

Isolated data

Design requirement

Access is managed through role-based permissions and data isolation. Sensitive data may not be entered into unapproved tools.

Traceable decisions

Design requirement

AI use, key outputs, source checks and the reasons for adopting an output are recorded, and tasks, work, revisions, feedback and capability are linked in one record. A student’s capability is shown through real evidence, and every judgement can be traced.

Human in the loop

Design requirement

AI can help produce suggestions, language scaffolding and first-round feedback. People make the decisions on issuing digital credentials, allocating opportunities, escalating risk and other high-impact judgements.

DATA AND ACCESS

Data minimisation and role-based access

A design rule for the product: collect only the data needed for a specific educational, task, service or governance purpose.

  • AI may read and process only the data a task needs
  • Schools, families and partners can see clearly what data is used for
