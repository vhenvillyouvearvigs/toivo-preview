WHY TOIVO

Motivation from real tasks.

Real tasks create real motivation. Information and content are within easy reach. What students often lack is a real problem worth committing to, where they can see the value they bring.

TOIVO puts students in front of real-world challenges, for example:

  • How can we help older people spot online scams and false health information?
  • How can we use data to improve energy use, transport or waste on campus?
  • How can we design support that helps new students from different backgrounds settle into school?
  • How can we tell Hong Kong’s cultural stories to different audiences in the city’s two written and three spoken languages?
  • How can we propose services for an NGO, social enterprise or company that are more empathetic and more workable?
  • How can we use AI to solve problems while protecting privacy, fairness and human dignity?

When students know their task has a real audience, concrete constraints and a chance of feedback from a partner, their commitment comes from:

  1. A problem I care about
  2. A next step I can take
  3. Progress I can see
  4. A real contribution to others

THE TOIVO PATHWAYS METHOD

The loop, the assessment and the credential.

Three parts. The first is how a Challenge is run, the second is how the work is judged, the third is what the student walks away with. Each carries its real status.

01

The five-step loop

IN DEVELOPMENT · 2026-08

Brief, Build, Feedback, Refine, Verify. Every step leaves one artifact, and the artifacts accumulate into an Evidence Pack. After the fifth step comes the next opportunity.

  1. 01Brief

    Define the problem, audience, limits and assessment criteria together with the partner.

    ARTIFACT
    Challenge Brief

  2. 02Build

    Research, interviews, bilingual comms, AI collaboration and a first prototype.

    ARTIFACT
    Problem Map

  3. 03Feedback

    The partner and beneficiaries respond at midpoint and again at final review.

    ARTIFACT
    Partner Feedback

  4. 04Refine

    Compare the feedback, revise, and explain every trade-off and judgement call.

    ARTIFACT
    Revision & Decision Log

  5. 05Verify

    Mentor and partner verify the work against the rubric. The pack is sealed and a digital credential is issued.

    ARTIFACT
    Evidence Pack

One standard, four domains, different scaffolding.

EVIDENCE PASSPORT

Growth you can see.

CONCEPT · 2026-09

TOIVO believes no student should be defined by a single score, exam paper, test or AI judgement.

We are designing the Evidence Passport so that students can bring these together:

  • What tasks I have completed
  • How I think, research and make choices
  • What mistakes I have made, and how I revised
  • What feedback I have had from teachers, mentors, partners and beneficiaries
  • What capabilities I have shown
  • What issues I care about
  • How I work with others and create value
  • What I want to explore next

Each Challenge leaves one Evidence Pack. The Evidence Passport gathers the evidence from many Challenges into a single record.

Students can show how they have grown through real work, process and reflection.

WHAT TOIVO BUILDS ON

Design grounded in learning science.

TOIVO’s system and content design draws on learning science, cognitive science, developmental psychology, motivation theory, neuroscience and responsible AI principles.
