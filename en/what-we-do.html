WHY TOIVO

Motivation from real tasks.

Real tasks create real motivation. Information and content are within easy reach. What students often lack is a real problem worth committing to, where they can see the value they bring.

TOIVO puts students in front of real-world challenges, for example:

  1. 01 How can we help older people spot online scams and false health information?
  2. 02 How can we use data to improve energy use, transport or waste on campus?
  3. 03 How can we design support that helps new students from different backgrounds settle into school?
  4. 04 How can we tell Hong Kong’s cultural stories to different audiences in the city’s two written and three spoken languages?
  5. 05 How can we propose services for an NGO, social enterprise or company that are more empathetic and more workable?
  6. 06 How can we use AI to solve problems while protecting privacy, fairness and human dignity?

When students know their task has a real audience, concrete constraints and a chance of feedback from a partner, their commitment comes from:

  1. 01

    A problem I care about

  2. 02

    A next step I can take

  3. 03

    Progress I can see

  4. 04

    A real contribution to others

THE COMMON CORE

One standard, four domains, different scaffolding.

We do not stream young people by where they are from or which language they started in. Everyone works toward the same four domains. What differs is the language support, the case material, the task difficulty and how the work may be submitted.

TOIVO PASSPORT

Growth you can see.

TOIVO believes no student should be defined by a single score, exam paper, test or AI judgement.

PLANNED · 2026 Q4 PROTOTYPE

We are designing the TOIVO Passport so that students can bring these together:

  1. 01 What tasks I have completed
  2. 02 How I think, research and make choices
  3. 03 What mistakes I have made, and how I revised
  4. 04 What feedback I have had from teachers, mentors, partners and beneficiaries
  5. 05 What capabilities I have shown
  6. 06 What issues I care about
  7. 07 How I work with others and create value
  8. 08 What I want to explore next

Each task leaves one Evidence Pack. The TOIVO Passport gathers the evidence from many tasks into a single record.

Students can show how they have grown through real work, process and reflection.

WHAT TOIVO BUILDS ON

Design grounded in learning science.

TOIVO’s system and content design draws on learning science, cognitive science, developmental psychology, motivation theory, neuroscience and responsible AI principles.

How we design for learning

Attention
Tasks have a clear purpose, a manageable load and a low-distraction way in
Active engagement
Students predict, ask questions, explain, create, discuss and make decisions
Feedback on errors
Errors are used for diagnosis and revision. Students are not shamed or labelled for them.
Consolidation
Learning is consolidated through spaced review, retrieval practice, teaching others and applying it in new contexts
Metacognition
Students learn to plan, monitor and evaluate their own learning strategies
Autonomy, competence, relatedness
Tasks offer choice, achievable challenges and support from real people
Authentic context
Knowledge is connected to daily life, community, culture, industry and social issues
Recovery and rhythm
The platform protects focus, rest, digital boundaries and a sustainable pace of learning
Identity and future self
Students explore “who I want to become” in a way they can keep revising
Social contribution
Students’ capability and work create real value for others

After “Did you get it right?”, we go on to ask: How did you think it through? How did you revise? What did you learn? Where can you use it? Who did it help?
